Learn the room. Then translate.

I'm Nafi. I grew up in Dinajpur, Bangladesh, left home at twelve for military school, and have been learning new rooms ever since. Today I live in Land O' Lakes, Florida and work remotely as a Solutions Engineer at Dasseti, translating between investment teams and their due-diligence software.

Before that: BlackRock, SkyBridge, Mayo Clinic. Studied Data Science at Luther College (GPA 3.5). On this site I write about how capital moves, build small tools I wanted, and write up match notes from Florida cricket.
