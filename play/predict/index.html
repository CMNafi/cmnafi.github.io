How scoring works

Keep it simple.

F1: 25 points for the correct winner, 18 for the correct second-place driver, and 15 for the correct third-place driver. Each driver must appear once. A correct driver in the wrong position earns zero. Cricket: 10 points for the correct match winner. Picks lock at the scheduled start. Draws, ties, abandoned games, and missing results remain unscored.
