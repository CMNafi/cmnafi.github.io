What does diversification change?

Three illustrative sleeves. Drag an allocation to rebalance the other two proportionally.

Assumptions and calculation

Illustrative annual returns: 6.5%, 9%, 6%. Volatility: 10%, 18%, 12%. Pairwise correlations: 0.65, 0.40, 0.50. Risk-free rate: 3%. The first sleeve already contains 60% public stocks and 40% bonds. Its preset allocates the entire portfolio to that sleeve. Private Credit Heavy is a blended sleeve proxy, not a separately modeled credit asset.

Variance is wᵀΣw; Sharpe is (return minus risk-free rate) divided by volatility. Correlation is the allocation-weighted average between distinct sleeves, undefined for one sleeve. The curve is the non-dominated feasible envelope on a 2% long-only grid. Drawdown proxy is max(0, 2 × volatility minus return), not an estimated historical maximum or a forecast. These are assumptions for learning, not market observations or investment advice.
