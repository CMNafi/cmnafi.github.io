The investment adviser landscape

Read the entity, then the numbers.

A brand can contain many advisers. Compare exact legal entities without confusing their regulatory assets with group AUM.

No matching manager. Try another name.

Official Form ADV snapshots, checked October 14, 2026. Figures cover each named legal adviser and its filing perimeter, not the parent group's assets. Item 5.F reports regulatory AUM; Item 5.D reports client types. Fee schedules require separate Part 2 review and remain unverified. No 13F holdings are loaded.
