At twelve I left home for Rangpur Cadet College, a military school. It is 66 km from Dinajpur. At that age it felt much further.

The rules were written down. How the place actually worked was not, and nobody explained it. I watched the people who had been there longer and tried to do what they did.

Most of what I learned there was ordinary: keep to a routine, and keep turning up when it stops being interesting. I still lean on that.
