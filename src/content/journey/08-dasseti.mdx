In 2023 I joined Dasseti, which makes due diligence software for investors. I work remotely as a solutions engineer. I am not a software engineer.

The job sits between two groups. Investment and operations teams need to evaluate managers, collect data and keep a record they can stand behind. The product can do a lot of that, if the problem is described to it properly. I learn how a client's team works, then explain each side to the other.

I wanted to work at a smaller company to be closer to the people using the software. Sitting with them shows me quickly when my own thinking is vague.

It is the same thing I have been doing since Rangpur: work out how a place runs, then try to be useful in it.
