At sixteen I moved to Changshu, China, to attend a United World College. I went on a full scholarship. I didn't speak the language.

Neither did many of my classmates. They came from more than a hundred countries, and most of us were new at the same time, which helped.

The school put people with very different backgrounds in the same classrooms and expected us to work it out. I learned that people I disagreed with usually had reasons, and that asking was faster than guessing.

We studied for the IB Diploma, which makes you carry several subjects at once. I liked that more than I expected.
