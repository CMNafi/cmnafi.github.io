After graduating I joined BlackRock in Wilmington, Delaware, as an analyst in Data and AI.

Yes, the same BlackRock I had confused with Blackstone.

I worked on risk reporting, fund performance metrics and capital-flow dashboards, around the Aladdin platform.

What I noticed most was that the best systems there were not exciting. They were dependable. They ran every day, at a scale I had not seen before, and people trusted them. That changed what I look for in software.
