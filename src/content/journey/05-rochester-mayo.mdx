My first real data work was at Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minnesota, where I was an AI intern from 2021 to 2022. I worked on data pipelines and reporting for research teams.

Two things stayed with me. A report that runs correctly every morning is worth more than something clever that works once. And the data was always messy. Cleaning it was most of the work, not a step before it.

The clinicians' questions were important and often vague. Before I could measure anything, I had to understand what they were really asking. That was the part I enjoyed most.
