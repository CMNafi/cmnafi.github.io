At SkyBridge I was a data scientist supporting manager selection. I helped build screens across more than 200 hedge fund profiles, and dashboards that showed exposure by strategy, geography and liquidity. I also contributed to investment memos.

I expected the models to matter most. They didn't. What mattered was whether someone could read the output in a meeting and decide something.

I learned that a memo is an argument, not a list of findings, mostly by reading the good ones.
