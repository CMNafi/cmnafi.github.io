I grew up in Dinajpur, in the north of Bangladesh. In our house, if you were bored, you were told to find a book. When you finished it, you found another.

It was a small place. Everyone knew everyone, and most days looked alike. I didn't have to work anything out there, which I only noticed after I left.

I think it is where I learned to pay attention, mostly because there was time to.
