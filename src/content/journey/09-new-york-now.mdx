I live in Land O' Lakes, Florida, and work remotely for Dasseti. Outside work I do three things.

**Field Notes.** I write about how capital moves: [GameStop's bid for eBay](/field-notes/gamestop-bid-for-ebay), Uber's $32.8 billion hole, [the 56,000-plus advisers in Form ADV](/field-notes/adv-universe). I start from a big number and try to find out what is behind it.

**The Garage.** Small tools I wanted and couldn't find, like a cricket fielding planner and a way to read an investment adviser's filings in one place. I build them with AI coding tools. My part is describing clearly what should exist.

**Cricket.** I play league cricket in Florida as an all-rounder and bowl right-arm fast. I write up the close finishes.
