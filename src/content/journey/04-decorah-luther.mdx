University took me to Luther College in Decorah, Iowa, a town of about eight thousand people. Winter there teaches patience. A small campus teaches you that depth beats breadth, if you let it.

I majored in Data Science with a minor in Computer Science and graduated in 2022. The degree gave me tools, but the bigger change was in the questions I asked. Where did this information come from? What is signal and what is noise? What decision is it actually supposed to support?

Junior year, I applied to Blackstone. Or I thought I did. When the interview invitation arrived, I read the name three times. **BlackRock.** I had prepared private equity case studies and was about to walk into a conversation about fixed income risk. I learned what duration meant very quickly.

That mix-up sent me down a rabbit hole I have never fully climbed out of: how money is actually organized, and why two firms with nearly identical names ended up as opposites. I eventually wrote it all down in [a Field Note](/field-notes/blackstone-vs-blackrock).
