For university I went to Luther College in Decorah, Iowa, a town of about 8,000 people. The winters are long. I studied Data Science with a minor in Computer Science and graduated in 2022.

In my junior year I applied to Blackstone. Or I thought I did. When the interview invitation arrived, it said **BlackRock.** I had prepared private equity case studies and was about to walk into a conversation about fixed income risk. I learned what duration meant very quickly.

I was embarrassed enough to go and find out why two firms with almost the same name do such different things. I wrote that up in [a Field Note](/field-notes/blackstone-vs-blackrock).
